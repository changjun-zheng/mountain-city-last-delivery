# EXP-02A street and room graphics

Original Phaser Canvas graphics extend the EXP-01 timber, roof, window, lantern, character and bridge vocabulary. No third-party bitmap, old side-view PNG or purchased asset is used. Two 3300×1300 outdoor maps and three 1100×650 interiors are drawn in current world coordinates; the 1100×650 logical viewport, actor scale 0.95 and camera zoom 1 are unchanged.

The street names, water-wave / mountain marks, joinery clasp, furniture and connections are provisional game inventions. They make no claim about real Hongyadong facilities or cultural history. Art and content remain subject to owner review.
